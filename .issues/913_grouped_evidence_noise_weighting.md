## Why

EasyPPO's noise-normalized critic regression decomposes into closed-form statistics that are consumer-agnostic: the gradient second moment of any squared-loss estimator on grouped noisy targets is `‖h‖²[pred_err² + Var(R|group)]`, so as fit improves the NOISE dominates; weighting by `1/max(σ̂,ε)` equalizes contributions and — for exogenous weights (functions of the group, not the outcome) — **preserves the estimation optimum**. The workspace aggregates grouped evidence everywhere (fix-trajectory groups, telemetry epochs, cal slices, per-zone outcome groups) but ships NO estimation-side weighting: `best_belief_score(s: u32, f: u32, ε)` takes integer counts only, `rating::update_*` carries a fixed `STANDARD_K`, and the reflex probe-size floors lack the closed form. Selection-side uncertainty (Beta-LCB/Wilson) already ships — estimation-side weighting composes WITH it (the invariance law is the license).

**Endogeneity caveat (verdict round 1, load-bearing):** the invariance law licenses weighting only where the weight is independent of the outcome given the group. EasyPPO's own setting is within-group own-target loss weighting (tolerant of the same-group plug-in σ̂, as the paper acknowledges); CROSS-group pooling — what T3/T4 do — requires σ̂ from an independent source (prior epoch / leave-one-out / design-level), because for Bernoulli outcomes the plug-in σ̂ is a deterministic function of the count being aggregated. See T3/T4 for the enforced scope + the plug-in negative control.

All four primitives are pure math, property-testable, zero-alloc, opt-in behind one feature flag (`grouped_evidence_weighting`), no domain semantics — katgpt-core substrate tier.

## Tasks

- [x] **T1 — `variance_floor(delta: f32, n: u32) -> f32`** (Popoviciu-derived `ε = Δ/(2√n)`): the minimum credible σ from n bounded draws. Property test: the extremal two-point distribution achieves it; monotone decreasing in n. Consumers: reflex per-suite probe floors (the Bench-066 tripwire's missing closed form), the GRPO ε-floor (riir-train Plan 431 T3.2), T3/T4 below.
- [x] **T2 — `filter_bias_bound(gamma: f32, p_not_c: f32) -> f32`** (`2Γ·P(¬C)`): the maximum bias any conditionally-filtered readout can carry (EasyPPO Eq 24; one-line proof from the censoring decomposition). A sibling DISCLOSURE floor for the Report-the-Floor law: any filtered readout prints its bias budget. Property test: sweep P(¬C) ∈ [0,1] against adversarial two-point rewards; tightness at the Bernoulli extreme.
- [x] **T3 — `best_belief_score_weighted`** (fractional-count Beta) — **σ̂-independence is load-bearing (verdict round 1)**: `α ← α + Σ_g w_g·k_g`, `β ← β + Σ_g w_g·(n_g−k_g)` with floored (T1), mean-one-normalized weights; LCB via the existing Newton/LUT machinery; **Kish `n_eff = (Σw)²/Σw²` disclosed** on every readout. **Scope of the exogenous-weight invariance law** (argmin preserved for `w ⊥ outcome | group`): (i) WITHIN-group own-target estimation (EasyPPO's own setting — the weight reshapes a group's own loss toward its own mean; the paper itself acknowledges the same-group plug-in σ̂ "can introduce bias but works well in practice" there); (ii) CROSS-group pooling — as here — ONLY with σ̂ from a source independent of the counts being aggregated: a prior epoch, leave-one-out, or a design-level variance. **Same-rate condition (verdict round 2):** argmin preserved iff the groups' true rates p_g are homogeneous within the arm; under heterogeneity the weighted Beta estimates the w-weighted estimand `Σw_g·n_g·p_g / Σw_g·n_g`, not the unweighted one — DISCLOSE which estimand a readout carries. **NEVER the pooled counts' own plug-in σ̂**: for Bernoulli outcomes σ̂_g = √(p̂_g(1−p̂_g)) is a deterministic function of k_g — fully endogenous; groups near the extremes get small σ̂ → floored to the LARGEST weight → the pooled rate is biased toward the extremes, exactly the case the negative control condemns. GOAT: (a) the negative control runs with the **plug-in σ̂** (the realistic mistake), not a synthetic endogenous weight, and MUST show the extreme-pulling bias — proving the gate bites and documenting the failure mode; (b) the independent-source arm beats unweighted on best-arm identification over synthetic heteroscedastic groups, paired LB95 > 0; **Report-the-Floor applies** (any interval readout benchmarks against `ConformalIntervalCalibrator<SeasonalNaiveForecaster>` on CRPS/coverage/Winkler). G4 alloc-free; G3 byte-identical at `w ≡ 1`.
- [x] **T4 — noise-scaled rating K**: `K_eff(g) = K/max(σ̂_g, ε)` per-call variant beside `rating::update_f32`/`update_scored` (signatures already thread K). **σ̂ from the group's history/prior epoch — never the current match sample** (same endogeneity law as T3: a σ̂ derived from the outcome being rated is endogenous and biases the asymptote the T3 law is supposed to preserve).
