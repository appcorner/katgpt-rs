## The math (corrected per the verdict review — the round-1 text carried two false claims)

- **Thinning** (Prop A.2) is exact ONLY as a transition on a Dirichlet-distributed state: X ~ Dir(α), independent B_i ~ Beta(ρα_i, (1−ρ)α_i) ⇒ Y_i = B_iX_i/ΣB_jX_j ~ Dir(ρα). It is **NOT mean-preserving on a fixed vector p** — E[B_i·p_i/ΣB_j·p_j] ≠ p_i (Jensen bias toward uniform; verified by simulation: p=(0.9,0.07,0.03), c=2, ρ=0.5 ⇒ E[Y]=(0.847,0.103,0.051)). The per-coordinate variance ratio under a Dirichlet input is **(c+1)/(ρc+1)**, NOT ρ (Var goes p(1−p)/(c+1) → p(1−p)/(ρc+1)).
- **The explore dial done exactly**: given a fixed belief vector p, draw directly from **Dir(c·p)** — mean exactly p by construction. This is the operator for "K noisy copies of a categorical belief"; no thinning involved.
- **Dirichlet-EMA / belief memory** (Prop E.1 + Cor E.1): memory weights L ~ Dir(ε·shares) over the last M predictions. **E[L_j] = share_j — deterministic and ε-independent** — a *schedule-weighted average* (an EMA specifically under a geometric schedule); Var[L_j] = w(1−w)/(εC+1) → 0 as ε→∞ (deterministic weights) and → categorical single-draw as ε→0 (resample-from-history). A finite ring TRUNCATES the infinite EMA tail (the L_{i,M} residual has no plain-EMA counterpart) — named, not hidden.
- **Concentration param**: c = 1/ν − 1 with ν ∈ (0,1) the variance fraction (bounded, designer-facing).
